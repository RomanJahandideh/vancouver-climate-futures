Vancouver Climate Futures

An interactive exploration of local climate choices and outcomes for three real Vancouver places: the False Creek foreshore, a lower-tree-canopy neighbourhood like Marpole, and the city's older rental-housing stock.

Grounded in real, cited data: the City of Vancouver's own Coastal Flood Risk Assessment (4.6m flood construction level, ~1m of sea level rise projected by 2100), the real 2021 BC heat dome (619 deaths province-wide, the deadliest weather event in BC history) and its documented ~20°C neighbourhood tree-canopy temperature gap, and the City's real RARA building-retrofit grant program. Every outcome you see is computed from your choices by a deterministic model, not decided by AI.
