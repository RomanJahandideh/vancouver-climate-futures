Okanagan Climate Futures

An interactive exploration of local climate choices and outcomes for three real Kelowna-area places: the Knox Mountain / Glenmore wildland-urban interface, the South East Kelowna agricultural benchlands, and the Okanagan Lake foreshore.

Grounded in real, cited regional climate projections (Pacific Climate Impacts Consortium, in partnership with the North Okanagan, Central Okanagan & Okanagan-Similkameen Regional Districts, Climate Projections for the Okanagan Region, Feb 2020) and Okanagan Lake's real regulated operating band (BC government Okanagan Lake Regulation System factsheet). Every outcome you see is computed from your choices by a deterministic model, not decided by AI.
